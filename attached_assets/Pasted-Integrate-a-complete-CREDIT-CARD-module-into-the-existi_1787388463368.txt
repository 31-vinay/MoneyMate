5. BILLING-CYCLE LOGIC
==================================================

IMPORTANT ACCOUNTING RULE FOR THIS PROJECT:

The individual credit-card transactions belong to a specific billing cycle.

However, for MoneyMate's monthly financial totals:

The TOTAL credit-card expense should be recognized in the month in which the credit-card STATEMENT / BILLING CYCLE is generated.

Example:

Billing Cycle:
5 Aug – 4 Sep

Statement Generated:
5 Sep

Transactions inside cycle:

Food ₹1,000
Shopping ₹2,000
Healthcare ₹1,500

Statement Total:
₹4,500

MoneyMate should:

- keep each individual transaction for category analysis
- show their categories in the Expense page
- classify each into Needs/Wants
- associate all of them with the September billing cycle

For monthly financial totals:

September Credit Card Expense = ₹4,500

Do NOT separately add every transaction plus the bill total.

The statement total is the single financial total for the billing cycle.

The individual transactions are the breakdown of that same ₹4,500.
